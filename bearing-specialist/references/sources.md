# Source register

Access checked: 2026-09-30. These are starting sources, not a complete rulebook.
Only the stated passages were checked; other chapter locations are navigation
aids from the contents. Reopen the relevant passage before using it in a finding.
Printed page numbers are used below. Unknown publication dates remain unknown.

Shigley's is the only textbook source. Manufacturer documents are primary
sources for their own products and guidance; Shigley's is a teaching reference,
not primary research. No other textbooks or academic papers are included.

## SHIG-01 · Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design

- **Authors:** Richard G. Budynas and J. Keith Nisbett; McGraw Hill.
- **Edition located:** 11th edition, SI Units, © 2020; ISBN 9789813158986.
- [Official publisher page and contents](https://www.mheducation.com.au/shigley-s-mechanical-engineering-design-11th-edition-si-units-9789813158986-aus-group)
- **Checked:** publisher's bibliographic information and contents only.
- **Find topics:** Chapter 11, Rolling-Contact Bearings; Chapter 12, Lubrication
  and Journal Bearings. Chapters 3, 4, and 7 cover loads/stresses, deflection,
  and shafts respectively.
- **Status:** **Located only** for engineering rules. The actual bearing chapters
  were not read; no equation, design rule, or page citation is verified from them.
- **Use:** foundational explanations after reading the relevant passage in an
  accessible copy. Record that copy's exact edition; do not transfer page numbers
  or numerical examples between editions. Publisher metadata alone is insufficient.

## SCH-01 · Schaeffler — Technical principles

- [Official MH 1 medias PDF](https://www.schaeffler.com/remotemedien/media/_shared_media/08_media_library/01_publications/schaeffler_2/catalogue_1/downloads_6/mh1_technical_principles_de_en.pdf)
- **Edition:** MH 1 medias; publication date not established.
- **Checked:** contents; bearing functions p. 4; fit criteria p. 13.
- **Supported guidance:** fit selection considers ring support, creep prevention,
  axial displacement where required, and assembly. Fits and ring temperature
  differences affect internal clearance. The passage distinguishes arrangements
  that permit displacement inside the bearing.
- **Find topics:** arrangements pp. 6–12; fits pp. 13–15; clearance pp. 16–21;
  seating tolerances p. 22; lubrication pp. 34–38; seals pp. 40–45.
- **Limit:** do not turn guidance for a particular shaft arrangement into a rule
  that all mechanisms need the same number or arrangement of bearings.

## Choosing sources

Start with SKF-01, NSK-01, or NTN-01 for general selection questions. Use SCH-01
for arrangement and mounting questions, THK-01 for cross-roller research, and
TIM-01 for relevant metals-industry examples. This routing is an editorial aid,
not a manufacturer ranking or a recommendation of bearing type.

The verified manufacturer passages mainly cover rolling-element bearings.
Shigley's Chapter 12 provides a route to journal-bearing study, but its contents
have not yet been read. This set does not establish verified coverage of plain
bearings, dedicated slewing-ring design, or directly consulted standards.
Locate appropriate primary documents if the application calls for those topics;
do not stretch the existing sources to cover them.

## Product-specific specifications

No product has been selected, so no individual product spec sheet is registered.
For an identified bearing, retrieve its manufacturer's current product page,
datasheet, dimensional drawing, and applicable mounting instructions. Record the
full designation, revision, units, definitions, and footnotes in the case evidence
